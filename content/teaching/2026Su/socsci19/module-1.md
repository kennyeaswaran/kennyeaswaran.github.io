## Further reading/watching

- Wired, [Professor (Graham Morehead) Answers AI Questions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-u6Ektrvk2U), 23 minutes (March 25, 2025) - he discusses a remarkably similar set of issues to the ones I'm covering throughout the whole quarter, so this might be a good sneak peek! (Though I disagree with him about consciousness)
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy - [Artificial Intelligence](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/artificial-intelligence/) (July 12, 2018). This is a very long and detailed article that will likely take longer than this whole class to go through! Ask me about any of it.
- Philosophers Daniel Dennett and Hubert Dreyfus [on PBS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2ziI2klMm30) after Garry Kasparov's loss to Deep Blue in 1997 (13 minutes) - they argue about whether embodiment and being neural are important for intelligence
- [Don Woodlock: Different Types of AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUncJdqwB8c), 8 minutes (Jan. 11, 2024)
- [Michael Wooldridge - What is Artificial Intelligence?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D2JY38VShxI), 10 minutes (Dec. 3, 2023) - video introduction to his Christmas Lecture
- IT Convergence - [Top Use Cases of AI-based recommendation systems](https://www.itconvergence.com/blog/top-use-cases-of-ai-based-recommendation-systems/). (March 8, 2023), text - Brief blogpost describing some possible uses
- [Mateo Ciocarlei - Embodied Intelligence](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXGAf3pqNDc), 4 minutes (Sep. 2, 2022) - more discussion of embodied intelligence
- Razor Science Show, [Biocomputers made from human brain cells could run the AI systems of the future](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-0GE4jJW7U), 19 minutes (Dec. 28, 2024)
- Overviews of current (March 2026) AI models:
    - [Matthew Berman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I0me2uEbfuE) - discusses text-generation, image generation, coding, speech/sound/music generation, video generation, and "world generation", as well as open-source models
    - [Tina Huang](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DsKZpgoy830) - mostly focuses on chatbots, but discusses the different levels of model within each company - flagship, lite, and medium, as well as some discussion of open-source
