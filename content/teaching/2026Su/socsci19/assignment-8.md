Look again at the assignment for the [Final
Project](/teaching/2026Su/socsci19/final-project/). For this assignment you
will put together a detailed outline of your plan for the final presentation,
and a first draft of a creative part of it. At the top of the file you should
say what sort of format you are doing, whether it's several short videos, a
lecture presentation, a game, or something else.

You will submit two files. One will be a detailed outline of the presentation.
The other will be a first draft of an attempt at some creative part of it.

## The outline

The outline should be fairly detailed. If you're doing a video or a game, you
should have an idea of what scenes you will have and roughly how long they are.
If you're doing a presentation, you'll want a detailed breakdown of how long
you want to spend on each part (e.g., will you spend 30 seconds explaining the
outline, then 3 minutes re-telling the story, then 2 minutes talking about what
your story illustrates about the importance of NVidia GPUs, and so on, or will
you begin with a 30 second description of one character in your story and then
give a 60 second background on AI images and then spend 2 minutes talking about
how they're connected, and so on?)

Make sure that your outline includes space (part of the presentation if a
presentation, or perhaps an attached document if some other format) to talk
about all the ways that you used AI in making your presentation, whether that's
in brainstorming ideas, generating images, editing video, converting text to
speech, anything else. Depending on your presentation topic, you might also
talk about how this use is connected to your fiction.

## The creative part

The creative part should be an ambitious attempt to do something that might not
work out. Maybe try to generate an AI video for part of it. Or use AI to write
a program that will do something interesting in your presentation. Or record a
video of you and have a AI system edit it. The point of AI tools is to do
something you couldn't do without AI, so if you're going to use one, try it out
now. And if you're not using AI, try making a version of one part of what
you'll do. You won't be graded on succeeding, just on trying.
