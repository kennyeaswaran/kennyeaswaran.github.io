Look at the following questions and think about them while reading or watching
your [assigned fiction](/teaching/2026Su/socsci19/assigned-fictions/). Then
write up the answers to these questions (probably about a paragraph each) and
submit the answers as a file here. **Do not use AI for this assignment!** You
must actually read or watch this yourself and form your own opinions. In later
weeks we will compare this with AI results.

Make sure that you provide enough information so that the students giving
feedback can understand what you are saying, even though they have probably not
read or watched your assigned fiction. Make sure that you indicate which
paragraph is an answer to which question.

## The questions

1. What is the name of your fiction? What year is it from? Give a brief (3-4 sentence) summary of whatever overview of the fiction will help someone understand your answers to the other questions.
2. Does the "artificially intelligent" being in your fiction appear to be a general-purpose artificial intelligence (AGI) or a special-purpose one? If it is special-purpose, does it appear to identify/classify, recommend, generate, or "do"? It might not be totally clear from the story, or some of the characters may believe something false about it. Please include a few sentences explaining this if you think there's something to explain.
3. Does the "artificially intelligent" being in your story appear to be agentic AI or tool AI? Does it have goals and interests of its own that it pursues with agency, or does it primarily react to the commands of others? Are there any changes in its level of agency through the story, or do any characters importantly mis-perceive it? Does the agency or lack thereof affect the story - would the story work just as well as a story if it had been the other way?
4. Does the "artificially intelligent" being in your story appear to be embodied or disembodied? Does this embodiment or disembodiment affect anything that it does in the story - could it equally well have worked the other way?
5. Does the "artificially intelligent" being in your story appear to be a symbolic system, that is intelligently programmed in order to follow specific instructions, or does it appear to be a neural net, that has been given some sort of training data and figured out what to do on its own? It may not be clear from the story what the source of its intelligence here, but you should write down something about what we might know, or the characters might believe.
