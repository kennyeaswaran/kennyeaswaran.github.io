## AI use summary

class.

### Identification/Classification

Other identification/recognition systems (Gemini summary):

- Biometric Authentication: Many respondents utilize face and fingerprint recognition for tasks such as unlocking mobile devices, authorizing payments, and accessing identity documents.
- Voice and Virtual Assistants: Several individuals rely on voice-activated systems like Siri, Alexa, and Echo dots for daily tasks.
- Image and Pattern Analysis: Users engage with AI-based tools for image searching, photo organization (such as pet identification), and verification services.
- Lack of Awareness or Usage: A significant number of participants reported no usage of additional AI-based identification systems or expressed uncertainty regarding whether the technologies they interact with qualify as such.

### Recommendation systems

Other recommendation systems (Gemini summary):

- Shopping and E-commerce: Respondents frequently identified platforms such as Amazon, as well as general shopping websites and fashion retailers, as sources for AI-driven purchase and product suggestions.
- Media and Entertainment: Services including Spotify, Apple Music, Netflix, Pinterest, TikTok, and the Steam "Discovery Queue" are commonly used for content and media discovery.
- Navigation and Productivity: Tools like Google Maps, Yelp, Uber Eats, LinkedIn, Handshake, and Teamwork Online are utilized for location services, food delivery, and professional networking or career development.
- Generative AI: Systems such as Claude, GPT, Grok, and Gemini were noted as tools for information and task assistance.
- Uncertainty or Non-usage: A large portion of respondents indicated they do not use, are unaware of using, or cannot recall using any additional AI recommendation systems.

### AI systems that "do" things

Other "do" systems (Gemini summary):

- Generative AI and Search Tools: Respondents frequently utilize large language models and assistant tools, specifically ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and AI-generated search summaries to answer questions and facilitate tasks.
- Smart Home and Automation: Participants leverage automated environmental systems, including smart thermostats that adjust to schedules and weather, smart lighting, and general home appliance controls.
- Personal Assistants and Vehicle Safety: Usage includes voice-activated assistants like Siri and Alexa for daily tasks, as well as automotive safety features such as lane-keeping and emergency collision detection.
- Gaming and Entertainment: Individuals engage with AI-driven opponents or non-player characters when playing video games.

### Large Language Model based generative AI systems

Other text-generating AI systems (Gemini summary):

- Commonly used AI tools: Users identified several external text-generating systems beyond standard options, most notably Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, and NotebookLM.
- Specialized and niche applications: Less frequently mentioned tools include ZotGPT, DeepSeek, Grok, Character AI, Doubao, Yuanbao, Grammarly, and TikTok AI, alongside voice-to-type software and virtual assistants like Siri and Alexa.
- Limited or no additional usage: The majority of respondents indicated they do not use any additional text-generating AI systems beyond those already covered in the survey.

### Non-text-based generative AI

Any other non-text-based generative AI systems (Gemini summary):

- Limited Use of Other Systems: A large majority of respondents (33 out of 47) indicated they use no other generative AI systems, or responded with 'No', 'None', 'N/A', or similar variations.
- Specific AI Systems Mentioned: Respondents listed Sora, Veo3, Nano Banana, mureka, and Krea (replacing Midjourney) as other systems used.
- Creative/Editing Software: Some responses mentioned software with AI features, including Adobe Photoshop, the photos app for creating collages (though unsure if it counts as AI), and powtoon (if it counts).
- Novelty/Limited-Scope Apps: One respondent noted using an app that turns people into dancing elves for Christmas, while another mentioned hearing that Sora is "bad."
