## Second Activity: Create "throwaway" e-mail address

Throughout this class, you will be asked to use various AI tools. Most of them
require you to create an account, which requires attaching an e-mail address. I
strongly recommend that you do not use your normal e-mail address.

Instead, you should generate a "throwaway" e-mail address that you use only for
signing up to random things. Why?

- See how much spam this e-mail address gets by the end of the term.
- If one of the services is a scam, it can't hack into your real account.
- You may have a "primary" account with one or more of these services, and with the "throwaway", you can see the difference between the pay and free tier, or based on accounts with different history

Any free e-mail provider is fine to use. Here are two reasonable options:

- [GMail](http://www.gmail.com) is convenient, because many services have a one or two click setup to create an account using a Google account, but it takes a bit more to use another e-mail address. (The one downside is that Google can still link your data from this "throwaway" account to your main account - but at least no one other than Google can.)
    - If you are currently signed in to GMail, then in the upper-right corner, there is either an icon with your initials or profile pic or the UCI anteater - click there, click "Add another account", click "create account", "for my personal use", enter at least three letters for a "first name" (preferably not your real one), and then pick a username (see below)
- [Proton mail](https://proton.me/) is well-known as a secure site that obeys Swiss privacy laws (some of the strongest in the world) - using this will keep your data completely secure, but will make signing into sites take a click or two extra.

Make sure the username you pick isn't identifiable as you - you can use
[this username generator](https://www.dashlane.com/features/username-generator)
for ideas if, like me, you aren't creative at coming up with usernames. (You can
tell each other your usernames if you want, or keep them secret.)

One thing to remember later in the quarter: some services have weekly usage
limit, but for each throwaway e-mail address you can create a new account and
get new usage up to the limit.

Make sure everyone in your group has created a "throwaway" e-mail address (and
has saved the login information somewhere!), and then move on to discussing the
survey about AI use.
