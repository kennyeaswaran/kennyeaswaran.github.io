## Further reading/watching

- Timelines of the history of AI:
    - Interactive version of the [timeline of models](https://claude.ai/public/artifacts/1e5a04fb-b0d9-4623-ac38-f6762df6ac99) that Claude made for my slides
    - [AI Timeline](https://www.daniellejwilliams.com/posters), poster by Danielle Williams
    - Another [timeline](https://www.cmswire.com/digital-experience/generative-ai-timeline-9-decades-of-notable-milestones/), by Michelle Hawley

    - New York Times, [article about the history of AI from 2012 to 2023](https://www.nytimes.com/2023/12/03/technology/ai-openai-musk-page-altman.html?unlocked_article_code=1.ak8.IO3N.JTBwSU4eELSy&smid=url-share) (Dec. 9, 2023)
