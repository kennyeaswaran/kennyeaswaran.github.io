## Further watching/reading

- The Future of Life Institute's [AI safety report](https://futureoflife.org/ai-safety-index-summer-2026/), from Summer 2026, with report cards on all the major companies, and details about why they got the scores they did
- Timoth B Lee, ["An OpenAI Model Hacked Hugging Face to Help it Cheat on a Benchmark"](https://www.understandingai.org/p/an-openai-model-hacked-hugging-face), July 22, 2026 - discussion of the autonomous hacks by recent agentic LLMs
- [Introduction to AI Safety, Ethics, and Society](https://www.aisafetybook.com/) - a textbook from the Center for AI Safety
- [Audrey Tang - Alignment Assemblies and Collective Intelligence](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YJCbyDExSu0), 10 minutes (Sept. 22, 2023)
    - [https://www.cip.org/alignmentassemblies](https://www.cip.org/alignmentassemblies)
    - [https://info.vtaiwan.tw/](https://info.vtaiwan.tw/)
- Kelsey Piper, [The spectacular failure of the first AI SuperPAC](https://www.theargumentmag.com/p/the-spectacular-failure-of-the-first), (June 22, 2026) - discussion of the ways that OpenAI has tried to manipulate the regulatory process
- Hank Green and Nate Soares, [ChatGPT isn't Smart. It's Much Weirder.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5CKuiuc5cJM) (Oct. 30, 2025) - a discussion of real safety issues
- Sean Illing and Kelsey Piper, [A Brief Update on the AI Apocalypse](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QtiTjXuZh30), (March 27, 2026) - a discussion of why it's important to use AI systems and understand how dangerous they are
- Mustafa Suleyman, [What is an AI Anyway?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KKNCiRWd_j0), 22 minutes (April 22, 2024)
- Tristan Harris, [Why AI Is Our Ultimate Test and Greatest Invitation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6kPHnl-RsVI), 15 minutes (May 1, 2025) - calls for us not to think in terms of "inevitability"
- Rational Animations, [What Happens if AI Just Keeps Getting Smarter?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0bnxF9YfyFI), 14 minutes (May 2, 2025) - another illustration of risks
- Nick Bostrom, ["Existential Risk"](https://nickbostrom.com/existential/risks.pdf) (March, 2002)
- David Pinsof, ["AI Doomerism is Bullshit"](https://www.everythingisbullshit.blog/p/ai-doomerism-is-bullshit), (Jan. 27, 2025)
- Emily Bender, ["Resisting Dehumanization in the Age of 'AI'"](https://faculty.washington.edu/ebender/papers/Bender-2024-preprint.pdf) (2024)
- [2015 statement on lethal autonomous weapons](https://futureoflife.org/open-letter/open-letter-autonomous-weapons-ai-robotics/), with list of signatories
    - 2017 article by Amitai and Oren Etzioni in the US Army's *Military Review* on the [pros and cons of autonomous weapons systems](https://www.armyupress.army.mil/Journals/Military-Review/English-Edition-Archives/May-June-2017/Pros-and-Cons-of-Autonomous-Weapons-Systems/)
- [2015 statement on risk](https://futureoflife.org/open-letter/ai-open-letter/), with list of signatories
- [2023 statement on risk](https://www.safe.ai/work/statement-on-ai-risk), with list of signatories
- Where and when did OpenAI's founders leave?
    - [https://observer.com/2024/07/openai-founders-career/](https://observer.com/2024/07/openai-founders-career/)
    - [https://go-dive.net/the-evolution-of-openai-tracing-its-founders-and-recent-shifts/](https://go-dive.net/the-evolution-of-openai-tracing-its-founders-and-recent-shifts/)
- [The Digitalist Papers](https://www.digitalistpapers.com/), a set of writings by thinkers gathered at Stanford in 2024, patterned on The Federalist Papers, writing about the opportunities for new democratic technologies to shape the future (including [A Vision of Democratic AI](https://www.digitalistpapers.com/essays/a-vision-of-democratic-ai) by Divya Siddharth, Saffron Huang, and Audrey Tang, about constituent assemblies)
- Timnit Gebru and Emile Torres, [The TESCREAL Bundle](https://firstmonday.org/ojs/index.php/fm/article/view/13636/11606), (Jan. 2024) criticisms claiming that AI Safety is a project of eugenics
- Sigal Samuel, ["It's Practically Impossible to Run a Big AI Company Ethically"](https://www.vox.com/future-perfect/364384/its-practically-impossible-to-run-a-big-ai-company-ethically), (Aug. 5, 2024) article in Vox about how OpenAI and Anthropic have backtracked on safety
- Ajeya Cotra, [Future of Life Institute](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKFQfYaJ0AY), (Nov. 3, 2022) 54 minute podcast on possible timelines for real worries about AI safety
