## Further watching/reading

- Clay Shirky, ["Students are Skipping the Hardest Part of Growing Up"](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/01/30/opinion/ai-social-skills-relationships.html)
- Hank Green, [Unfortunately You Need to Know What The Jevons Paradox Is](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a6sYYrLTOjQ), 33 minutes (July 6, 2026) - he starts with a long discussion of this paradox, but goes into much more about the future and our present in his distinctive style
- 80,000 Hours, [How not to lose your job to AI](https://80000hours.org/agi/guide/skills-ai-makes-valuable/), (June, 2025) - discussion of which skills might get more valuable as AI expands, rather than less valuable, and how to think about that when shaping your own job search, and job preparation
- Analysis of past automation transitions:
    - James Pethokoukis, [What the story of ATMs tells us about automation and jobs](https://www.aei.org/economics/what-atms-bank-tellers-rise-robots-and-jobs/), (June 6, 2016)
    - Ernie Tedeschi, [The decline of travel agents](https://www.stripeeconomics.com/p/the-decline-of-travel-agents), (April 21, 2026)
- How AI has or hasn't already shaped some jobs:
    - NYTimes, [Coding After Coders: The End of Computer Programming As We Know It](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/03/12/magazine/ai-coding-programming-jobs-claude-chatgpt.html?unlocked_article_code=1.gVA.SS-h.IBN_JdA2HLa2&smid=url-share) (March 12, 2026)
    - Deena Mousa, [The Algorithm Will See You Now](https://worksinprogress.co/issue/the-algorithm-will-see-you-now/) (why radiologists haven't been replaced by AI) (Sept. 25, 2025)
    - [At Amazon, Some Coders Say Their Jobs Have Begun to Resemble Warehouse Work](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/05/25/business/amazon-ai-coders.html?unlocked_article_code=1.a08.B6xQ.ckXJBDG5fJBs&smid=url-share), New York Times (May 25, 2025)
- Useful predictions of future AI progress on jobs:
    - Ezra Klein, [Why the AI Job Apocalypse (Probably) Won't Happen](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/03/opinion/ai-jobs-unemployment-silicon-valley.html?unlocked_article_code=1.gVA.wj8z.JVS4ZUWoZmyA&smid=url-share) (May 3, 2026)
    - Rational Animations, [When Will AI Automate All Mental Work, And How Fast?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ffmwR9PPVM), 12 minutes (May 31, 2025) - explains Tom Davidson's economic model that gives a best guess of taking 3 years, around 2040, though it could be as early as 2030 or as late as 2100
    - Financial Times, [AI is Transforming the World of Work, Are We Ready For It?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQX_wIW9Nh0), 17 minutes (Dec. 1, 2024)
    - New York Times, [AI Might Take Your Job. Here are 22 New Ones it Could Give You](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/06/17/magazine/ai-new-jobs.html?unlocked_article_code=1.a08.Tfm-.esw1bVUK-82Z&smid=url-share), (June 17, 2025)
    - New York Times, [Which Workers Will AI Hurt Most: The Young or the Experienced?](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/07/07/business/ai-job-cuts.html?unlocked_article_code=1.a08.ao6p.LFxSn5li-bcU&smid=url-share), (July 7, 2025)
- The past and future of exponential growth:
    - Our World in Data's post on [what is economic growth](https://ourworldindata.org/what-is-economic-growth), (May 13, 2021) and why we often think that it is about money and forget why it matters
    - Tom Murphy's blog post on [Galactic-Scale Energy](https://dothemath.ucsd.edu/2011/07/galactic-scale-energy/), (July 12, 2011) and how even a few centuries of the kind of growth we have so far will run up against it
    - Holden Karnofsky, [The Most Important Century,](https://www.cold-takes.com/most-important-century/) a series of blog posts (late 2021) arguing that everything is likely to change in unknowable ways in a few decades
        - [Everything Might Change This Century](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3K25VPdbAjU), 32 minutes (July 24, 2022) a video explaining Karnofsky's blog posts
- General predictions on AI power
    - Stuart Russell, [How will AI Change the World?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RzkD_rTEBYs), 6 minutes (Dec. 6, 2022)
    - Ray Kurzweil, [The Last Six Decades of AI and What Comes Next,](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uEztHu4NHrs) 13 minute (June 27, 2024) TED talk from a famous futurist who is always a bit out there
    - Geoff Hinton, [Will AI Outsmart Human Intelligence?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IkdziSLYzHw), 47 minutes (July 22, 2025)
