## Further watching/reading

- [More on the OpenAI-DeepSeek controversy](https://www.scbc-law.org/post/code-claims-and-consequences-the-legal-stakes-in-openai-s-case-against-deepseek)
- Anthropic
    - [The Claw Code fork](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/what-is-claw-code/) of Claude Code (copyright violation) (March 31, 2026)
    - Anthropic's announcement of [Glasswing](https://www.anthropic.com/glasswing) and Mythos (finding security hacks in other software) (April 7, 2026)
    - David Shapiro's independent [discussion of Glasswing](https://daveshap.substack.com/p/project-glasswing-anthropic-has-crossed) (April 8, 2026)
    - [Hank Green and Sherri Davidoff discuss Mythos and Glasswing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6pgZKVcKpw), 50 minutes (April 10, 2026)
- Past cases of technological IP change
    - [Mechanical licenses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_license) (1908)
    - [Napster](https://www.abortretry.fail/p/the-story-of-napster) (1999)
    - [Google Books](https://www.authorsalliance.org/2023/02/24/fair-use-week-2023-looking-back-at-google-books-eight-years-later/) (2008)
    - Memes and copyright - [in the US](https://news.temple.edu/nutshell/2023-03-20/what-do-you-meme-you-can-get-sued-social-post), and [in the UK](https://www.technollama.co.uk/do-memes-infringe-copyright)
- Shruti Rajagopalan, ["A for Effort: How AI Upends Copyright Law"](https://srajagopalan.substack.com/p/a-for-effort-how-ai-upends-copyright), (June 4, 2026) - an essay about how copyright protects text, not ideas, because historically text was the hard part to create but easy to copy, but that AI may change this now that text is easy to create
- [The Panama Playlists](https://panamaplaylists.com) (July 29, 2025) - Spotify accidentally leaked the listening habits of many politicians and celebrities
- [Privacy and Data Security with AI for Educators](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u61ehIBcC3g), 6 minutes (Dec. 13, 2023)
