This page has links to the text or video of the assigned fictions. Pick one in
this survey *(survey link removed)* in the first week of class.

Several later assignments will ask you to relate this fiction to the topics of
that module. You will do a final project about the fiction at the end of the
term (roughly a 10 minute video, or other creative idea).

The assignment spreadsheet *(link removed)* will show you which you have been
assigned to.

## Short Stories

- The Sorcerer's Apprentice (Der Zauberlehrling) by Johann Wolfgang von Goethe (1797)
    - [1955 translation by Edwin Zeydel](https://www.scottish-country-dancing-dictionary.com/sorcerers-apprentice.html), original text and [more contemporary translation](https://blogs.transparent.com/german/german-poetry-johann-wolfgang-von-goethe-der-zauberlehrling-the-sorcerers-apprentice/) by Sandra Rösner
    - [Performance](https://video.disney.com/watch/sorcerer-s-apprentice-fantasia-4ea9ebc01a74ea59a5867853) by Mickey Mouse in Fantasia (1940)
- [Maelzel's Chess Player](https://www.eapoe.org/works/essays/maelzel.htm), by Edgar Allen Poe (1836)
    - There is a 1927 [film](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0018045/) you might be able to track down, if you are interested
- [The Machine Stops](https://www.cs.ucdavis.edu/~koehl/Teaching/ECS188/PDF_files/Machine_stops.pdf), by E.M. Forster (1909)
- A Logic Named Joe, by Murray Leinster (1946) ([in HTML](https://www.baen.com/chapters/W200506/0743499107___2.htm)) ([in PDF](https://www.google.com/books/edition/A_Logic_Named_Joe/CZGV6h5fQzIC?hl=en&gbpv=1&printsec=frontcover) - this is a whole book, but the story itself is just 12 pages, you have to scroll a bit to find it)
- [The Great Automatic Grammatizator](https://gwern.net/doc/fiction/science-fiction/1953-dahl-thegreatautomaticgrammatizator.pdf), by Roald Dahl (1954)
- [The Last Question](https://users.ece.cmu.edu/~gamvrosi/thelastq.html), by Isaac Asimov (1956)
- Silicon Muse *(scan removed)*, by Hilbert Schenck (1985)
- [Learning To Be Me](https://gwern.net/doc/fiction/science-fiction/1995-egan.pdf), by Greg Egan (1990)
- [Cat Pictures Please](https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/kritzer_01_15/), by Naomi Kritzer (2015)

## Short Films

- [Slaughterbots](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9CO6M2HsoIA), by the Future of Life Institute (2017)
- Zima Blue, Season 1, Episode 14 of "Love, Death, and Robots" (2019)
    - Available on [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/watch/80223962)

## Feature Films

- Metropolis (1927)
    - Available [in restored length on Kanopy](https://www.kanopy.com/en/uci/watch/video/114333), likely also from other sources. Note that some sources have a different music track added, like [Giorgio Moroder's disco version from 1984](https://www.kanopy.com/en/uci/watch/video/114085).
- The Stepford Wives (1975)
    - Available at various sites with subscription or payment, or for free (with ads) on [Tubi](https://tubitv.com/movies/507433/the-stepford-wives)
- Smart House (1999)
    - Available on Disney+, or for $3.99 on Amazon. I haven't found a free site.
- Her (2013)
    - Available on Paramount+, or for $5.99 on Amazon. I haven't found a free site.

## Other works

- The 1870 ballet Coppélia, by Léo Delibes
    - Any performance of the ballet will be fine - here are some available in their entirety on YouTube:
    - [Bolshoi ballet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OtKWj1KaPD8), (2011) - traditional performance
    - [Victor Ullate Ballet de Madrid](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3xRba2dhGTw), (2013) - updated with a sci-fi theme
- Les Contes d'Hoffmann (Tales of Hoffmann), 1881 opera by Jacques Offenbach
    - The following are some good performances of the entire opera, available online for free with subtitles (only one scene is relevant, so I have indicated the times in each video for that scene)
    - [College of Charleston opera](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3n8QmaCnitk), (2023) (26:08-53:05)
    - [Monte Carlo Opera](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tw5wGG12M7k), (2022) (33:10-1:10:21) (subtitles in French, YouTube can auto-translate to English)
    - [Teatro Real de Madrid](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zmzxhI-F2PI), (2018) (47:18-1:26:10) (subtitles in Spanish)
    - [Compañía Nacional de Ópera Mexico](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WiZhEpYHPlA), (2021) (34:30-1:10:50) (subtitles in Spanish)
    - There are some delightfully over-the-top performances of the climactic aria
        - Two from the Metropolitan Opera's more recent production, one by [Kathleen Kim](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9emRjIMZsVk) in 2009, and one by [Erin Morley](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VXKsOeccz8w) in 2015.
        - [Natalie Dessay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R1zoNGUiGto) at the Vienna State Opera in 1993
        - [Elizabeth Futral](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbxWX5lSNTU) at the Florida Grand Opera in 2011
- The 2021 film Coppélia, by Maurizio Balagnini and the Dutch National Ballet
    - Film available through the UCI Library on [Kanopy](https://www.kanopy.com/en/uci/watch/video/12522233)
        - (In case you're interested, all three of these are adaptations of the story [The Sandman](https://www.ux1.eiu.edu/~rlbeebe/sandman.pdf) (Der Sandmann), by E.T.A. Hoffmann (1817))
- [Frankenstein](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/84/84-h/84-h.htm), by Mary Shelley (1818) (also many films)
- The character of the Sorting Hat, in all their appearances throughout the Harry Potter novels (1997-2007)
- The character of Janet, from throughout the TV show [The Good Place](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt4955642/) (2016-2020)
- [Detroit: Become Human](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1222140/Detroit_Become_Human/) (2020)
