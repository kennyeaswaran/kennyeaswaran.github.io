- Make sure the Zoom app is updated on your computer
- All the video quizzes in [Module 1](/teaching/2026Su/socsci19/module-1/)

For each module, do all the video quizzes **before** the meeting, then come to
the meeting on Zoom on **Tuesday/Thursday at 11 am**.

Zoom meetings will nearly all be small group activities in breakout rooms.
[Activity link](/teaching/2026Su/socsci19/current/).

You should be prepared to have your **camera on** and **microphone on**. (You
will use a virtual background, so no one can see if your room is messy.) If you
can't do this one week, let me know in advance. If you can't do this regularly,
then you should take the class next year, when it will be in-person.

The evening after each meeting, you will need to turn in a written assignment,
and then by the next meeting, give feedback on other students' written
assignments.

There is a **final project** (which could be an 8-12 minute presentation, a
computer game, AI-generated video, or some other creative work that takes about
10 minutes to interact with). This is due by **Monday, July 27** and you will be
assigned to give feedback on three others by **Wednesday, July 29**. This will
be about a [work of fiction](/teaching/2026Su/socsci19/assigned-fictions/) that
you will pick at the beginning and read or watch in the second module.

I recommend **going to the page for each module (from links above, or below) to
find the assignments** - these pages include links to versions of the videos
without quizzes, that you can skip ahead and back in if you want to review (or
if you want a hint while doing the quiz). These pages also have bonus links for
additional resources you might find interesting, if you want to learn more about
any topic.

## Schedule

The date listed is the date of the Zoom meeting, and video quizzes should be
completed before then. Written assignments are due on Friday, two days later,
and comments on others are due by the following Monday.

You can get half credit for late assignments.

In each video quiz, if you get a question wrong, you can re-do the quiz - but you
first have to finish watching through all of it, then hit "submit", and then
reload the assignment, and start again from the beginning.

I can grant an excuse for missing a Zoom meeting, or extensions on any video
quizzes or written assignments, if you let me know at least 24 hours **in
advance** that there is some time-sensitive activity that will interfere (e.g.,
you have to take your uncle to the airport, your best friend's poetry recitation
is on Tuesday at 11, your lab is doing field research all week); if you don't let
me know in advance, I will only grant credit or extensions if it's an official
university-excused reason (e.g. medical with a doctor's note).
