## Assignments and grading

Section and homework assignments are submitted
[on Gradescope](https://www.gradescope.com/courses/1406274/). Individual
assignment links will be added to the schedule as they are set up.
{: .note }

Section assignments must be done **in-person** during section, unless you let me
know **in advance** that you have to miss it, or can **provide documentation**
for an unexpected absence.

Homework assignments are due on Sunday at midnight, at the end of the week in
which it is listed.

Exams (all in EH 1200, the regular classroom):

- Midterm 1: Tuesday, Oct. 20, 2-3:20
- Midterm 2: Thursday, Nov. 12, 2-3:20
- Final: Thursday, Dec. 10, 1:30-3:30 pm

The final grade will be:

- Final exam: 40%
- Midterm 1: 20%
- Midterm 2: 20%
- Section assignments: 10%
- Homework: 10%

There is also 2.5% extra credit for optional participation in a study about the
Anteater virtues.
