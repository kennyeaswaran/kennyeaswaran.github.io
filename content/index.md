I am Professor in the [Department of Logic and Philosophy of Science](https://www.lps.uci.edu/)
at the [University of California, Irvine](https://www.uci.edu/).
{: .lede }

I work in formal epistemology, decision theory, and the philosophy of mathematics.
Much of my recent thinking is about analogies between individual and collective
decision making, with lessons to be drawn in both directions.

## Before Irvine

<img class="photo right" src="/images/2020-princeton-talk.jpg" alt="Kenny Easwaran in 2020, with red hair, holding a handout while giving a talk at Princeton">
<img class="photo left" src="/images/2018-06-texas-am.jpg" alt="Kenny Easwaran in 2018, with blue hair, on the Texas A&amp;M campus">

I got my PhD from the [Group in Logic and the Methodology of Science](http://logic.berkeley.edu)
at [UC Berkeley](http://www.berkeley.edu) in 2008.

I was a post-doctoral fellow in the [Philosophy Department](http://philrsss.anu.edu.au)
of the [Research School of Social Sciences](http://rsss.anu.edu.au) at the
[Australian National University](http://www.anu.edu.au/) in 2008 and 2009,
assistant professor in the [Philosophy Department](http://dornsife.usc.edu/phil/)
at the [University of Southern California](http://www.usc.edu) from 2009 to 2014,
and associate professor and then professor in the
[Philosophy Department](https://liberalarts.tamu.edu/philosophy/) at
[Texas A&M University](https://www.tamu.edu/) from 2014 to 2023.

I have taught many times at [Canada/USA Mathcamp](http://www.mathcamp.org),
and was Academic Coordinator there in the summer of 2010.
