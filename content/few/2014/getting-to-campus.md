## Getting to Campus

If you plan to drive, you can find
[parking information here](https://visit.usc.edu/maps-directions/university-park-campus/public-parking/).
Parking is $8 a day. It looks like the best bet is to exit the 110 at Exposition
Blvd (map here), and then enter campus at the gate along Exposition Blvd at Watt
Way, and ask the gate attendant for directions to a lot or garage. If you're
coming from the west, you could also exit the 10 at Vermont Ave going south,
then turn left at Exposition Blvd and endter campus at Watt Way (map here). You
may be a 10 or 15 minute walk from the conference site, which is the Mudd Hall
of Philosophy, labeled "Mudd" on this map. (Thanks to Marshall Abrams for making
these maps!)

Travel from downtown to USC takes about 12 minutes on the train, plus 2 minutes
walking from the station to the department, and a few minutes walking from your
hotel to the station in downtown (plus possibly time to buy a ticket). The train
runs every 12 minutes from 5 am until midnight (2 am on Friday and Saturday
night). Board at **7th and Flower**, take an **Expo Line** train towards
**Culver City**, go four stops to **USC/Expo Park**, and the philosophy
department is immediately there, with a tower and courtyard. The conference is in
[**room 106**](http://web-app.usc.edu/web/its/spaces/room-finder/details.php?id=103).

### Detailed instructions, with photos

Enter the station from the escalators on the north side of **7th St at Flower
St** or **7th St at Figueroa St**. (There is another entrance as well, but
connections from there to the departure platform are more complicated. Some
people will board at the much simpler station aboveground at **Pico and
Flower**.) If you don't have a TAP card or you don't have money left on it, you
will need to use the ticket vending machine to
[buy a card and load a $1.50 fare on it](http://www.metro.net/riding/fares/how-to-buy-1way-ride/).
(You can also load any amount of money if you plan to ride multiple times. If
you will ride at least 4 segments in a day, a $5 day pass is cheaper than paying
for each ride separately.)

Tap your card at the turnstile to get into the station. (You won't need it again
once you're in, so you can put it away.) Go down the stairs to get to the level
with the Expo Line (if you entered at Flower, stay right). Trains to multiple
destinations depart from this platform. Look for an **Expo Line train towards
Culver City**.

Ride the train to the **USC/Expo Park station** (4 stops, about 12 minutes). The
philosophy department is clearly visible from the train as you arrive at the
station. Here is an image of the building and train as it looked 60 years ago
and now: *(image not preserved)*

At the philosophy building, enter the courtyard. The department office is
immediately on your left if you need to do anything there. The conference will
be in **room 106**, which is inside, just past the department office, on the
left.

If you accidentally get off the train one stop too early or late, you will also
be at USC, and will need to walk across campus to the philosophy building, which
is labeled **MHP** or **Mudd** on
[campus maps](http://web-app.usc.edu/maps/map.pdf).

### From other locations

For people coming from further away on Metro, day passes will save money. If you
are near a subway station on the Red Line or Purple Line, take the subway to 7th
St, go up to the level where the Expo Line departs from (double-check which
staircase you go up to make sure you end up on the side of the platform where
people are waiting), tap your card again at the posts, and then follow the above
directions.

There are also other public transit options from other parts of the city,
notably the buses down Vermont Ave (204 or 754), Alvarado St and Hoover Ave
(200), and Figueroa St (81). You can find other options on Google Maps transit
directions, or by asking Kenny at easwaran AT usc DOT edu.

### Return

Return to downtown is very straightforward. The Metro station is in the street
right out front of the philosophy building. Make sure to tap your card at the
post with the sensor (you can load more money on it at the machine if needed),
and wait on the platform heading towards downtown Los Angeles. Most people will
ride all the way to the end, at 7th St, but some will be able to get off one
stop earlier at Pico.
